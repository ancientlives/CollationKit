# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in the CollationKit community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy
community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, disciplines and experiences. This project sits between computer
  science and textual scholarship, and both traditions have things to teach.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and arguing from evidence.
- Crediting the work and ideas of others, including prior scholarship.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, or unpublished research shared in confidence, without explicit
  permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer, [@ancientlives](https://github.com/ancientlives),
for example by requesting a private channel in a minimal issue that contains no details. All complaints will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not
align with this Code of Conduct. Responses range from a private warning, through a public warning or temporary
ban, to a permanent ban from the project, according to the severity and pattern of the behaviour.
